Information Architecture

While conducting our card sort, we noticed participants had similar needs that were grouped in the same way. Our participants assisted us in categorizing and eliminating certain topics we had in our card sort, and this is what guided the creation of our sitemap. In the sitemap, we grouped all items related to the room information off to the right but kept it under Room Reservation. This is because users felt these were information they would come across while reserving the room but that details such as room information, photos and number were its own thing and might be found on a secondary page off the “Create A Reservation” page. We’ve also grouped all things support related together, while removing ‘Tutorials’ as an option as participants felt it was not necessary due to us having a Chatbot and FAQ page. This is also reflected in our prototype, showing how users will interact with these two functionalities in giving them results they are looking for and seamless navigation on the portal.

Prototype #1

Our first prototype is for the "Book Again Task" this is a feature we've included in our design based on user needs. Our personas dislike the lengthy experience of not just booking a room but of also finding the right room and building. This feature will allow users to book a room they've previously booked if it's available. This feature also is also useful for any users who feel they have a favorite srudy room on campus. The pages depict the interfaces users see while performing this task.
